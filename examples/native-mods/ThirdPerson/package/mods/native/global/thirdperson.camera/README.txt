Third Person

Extract the ZIP contents into mods/native/global/thirdperson.camera in your DLUMA installation. If using the staged folder, copy the entire thirdperson.camera directory into mods/native/global. Enable Third Person in the Mods menu and restart Player.

T: toggle first person / third person
Hold Left Alt + mouse (Shoulder style): orbit around the player
Release Left Alt (Shoulder style): return to the shoulder view
Mouse (Free camera style): rotate the camera; movement follows the viewing direction
Alt + Left / Right: shift the camera sideways
Alt + Up / Down: raise / lower the camera
Alt + Page Up / Page Down: move closer / farther
Alt + Home: reset camera offsets

Camera offsets are saved automatically. Each game session starts in first person.
Camera style: choose Shoulder for Alt-held orbit, or Free camera to rotate with the mouse and move relative to the camera. While aiming in Free camera, movement strafes.
Free camera interactions: nearby visible objects can be used without turning the character toward them. Use the normal prompt and interaction key. Normal reach and obstruction checks still apply.
First person during special actions: briefly use first person for takedowns, grapples, dropkicks, grabs, and pounces.
Hold right click to aim. Aiming settings control the closer camera and magnification, or let you use first person while aiming. Releasing aim restores your saved camera distance.

Open Third Person in DLUMA's mod configuration to change controls and camera framing. Select a binding, choose Rebind or press Enter, then press a key or mouse button. Escape cancels the change; Reset restores its default.

Horizontal camera angle (degrees): rotate the camera around your character. Zero keeps the normal shoulder view.
Camera elevation (degrees): positive values raise the camera; negative values lower it. These angles combine with your saved keyboard offsets.
Adjustment speed (%): how quickly the camera moves while an adjustment key is held. 100% is the normal speed.
Orbit return time (ms): how long the camera takes to return after releasing orbit. 250 ms is a quarter of a second.
Smooth camera movement: smooth position follow and vertical movement around a stable shoulder-height target. Enabled by default; mouse look, orbit, and aim stay responsive.
Smoothing time (ms): how gradually the camera settles, from 20 to 300 ms. The default is 120 ms; higher values feel softer.

Camera collision adjusts automatically with smoothing enabled or disabled. The camera pulls in near obstacles and eases outward when space clears.

Disable Third Person in the Mods menu and restart Player to uninstall it. Its directory can then be removed.
